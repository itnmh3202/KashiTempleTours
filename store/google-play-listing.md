```
Experience the spiritual heart of Varanasi with Kashi Temple Tours.

Varanasi, also called Kashi, is one of the oldest living cities in the world. Local guide Santosh Kumar, with more than 20 years of experience, leads you through its most sacred temples and ghats and explains the meaning behind every ritual you see. The journey ends with the evening Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat.

THE TOUR: 5 SACRED STOPS, ABOUT 4.5 HOURS
• Shri Kashi Vishwanath Temple: one of the 12 Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva
• Kal Bhairav Temple: the fierce protector of Varanasi
• Vishalakshi Temple: one of India's sacred Shakti Peethas
• Sri Varahi Devi Temple: a rare shrine to one of the mother goddesses
• Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat: the famous evening fire ceremony on the river

WITH THIS APP YOU CAN
• See the full itinerary, with photos and timings for each stop
• Book the tour on Viator or GetYourGuide
• Chat with Santosh directly on WhatsApp to ask questions or arrange hotel or airport pickup
• Find the meeting point on the map
• Read what to wear, photography rules and other temple tips before you go
• Read reviews from other travellers

GOOD TO KNOW
• Private tour with a local guide
• Free cancellation up to 24 hours before the tour starts
• Admission tickets are not included
• Meeting point: St. Thomas Church (Girja Ghar), Luxa Rd, Varanasi, with pickup available on request

Contact Santosh Kumar on WhatsApp: +91 765 205 9003
Website: www.kashitempletours.com
```
