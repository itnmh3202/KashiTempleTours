Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy explains how the Kashi Temple Tours website (www.kashitempletours.com) and the Kashi Temple Tours Android app handle your information. The app displays this website, so this policy covers both.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and the website and app do not collect, store or sell personal information. We do not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies.

If you contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email, we receive whatever you choose to send, such as your name, phone number, travel dates and hotel. We use it only to answer your questions and arrange your tour, and we do not share it with anyone else.

Bookings

Bookings are made through third-party platforms such as Viator and GetYourGuide. When you book there, your information is handled under that platform's own privacy policy.

Third-party services

Some parts of the website are provided by other companies, which may receive technical information such as your IP address when the page loads:

  • Google Fonts: loads the typefaces used on the site.
  • Google Maps: shows the meeting point map.
  • WhatsApp, Viator, GetYourGuide and TripAdvisor: only when you tap a link to them.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Offline storage

To load faster and work offline, the website and app save copies of the site's own pages and images on your device. This contains no personal information, and you can clear it at any time in your browser or app settings.

Children

The website and app are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
